A Ministry Eliot Serves

Four Winds Professional Guild

Sending medical experts to high-risk, hard-to-reach regions of the world — providing surgical care, training, and logistics support in partnership with the local church.

Who They Are

Four Winds Professional Guild is a non-profit agency dedicated to sending medical experts — surgeons and other specialists — to the far reaches of the world, where the local church has the least access to essential care.

Every deployment partners with a church already present in that region, training local believers in the knowledge, skills, and infrastructure to sustain the work long after the guild's team has gone home. The aim isn't only medical: an enduring, Christ-centered presence is how the guild carries out its ministry, for the glory of God.

Mission

To glorify God by serving and promoting the local church worldwide — providing expertise, partnership, training, and logistics support for essential surgical services in areas of high risk.

Three Ways to Join the Work

Everything the guild accomplishes is by the grace and power of God — and it takes a body of people praying, going, and giving to sustain it.

Pray

Prayer is the foundation of everything the guild accomplishes in the field. Reach out through 4WPG's site to receive a monthly prayer letter with specific ways to pray for the work.

Go

Not everyone is called to leave home for the far reaches of the world — but if you are, short- and long-term service opportunities are open. Contact 4WPG through their site to learn more.

Give

Financial partnership funds the medical teams, equipment, and logistics that make each deployment possible.
