About

PathLight exists to bring clarity to organizations and the people leading them — untangling structure, sharpening decisions, and building the discipline that turns direction into results.

Eliot Rupe leads PathLight after two decades in Air Force Special Operations as a Pararescueman at the highest levels, retiring in 2026. He also serves as a teaching elder, work that often includes short-term biblical counseling for individuals, couples, and organizations.

Every engagement — consulting, leadership development, or nonprofit work — starts from the same conviction: Scripture is the actual operating system, not an add-on.

Eliot currently serves as COO of Four Winds Professional Guild, a nonprofit — bringing that same day-to-day operating experience into PathLight's nonprofit consulting work.
